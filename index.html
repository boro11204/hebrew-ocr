How to get your free key (2 minutes)
  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Create API key (if asked, pick or create a project — any name).
  3. Copy the key (it starts with AIza or, for newer keys, AQ.) and paste it above, then Save key.

The free tier needs no credit card. You can only be charged if you yourself turn on billing for that project in AI Studio. Without billing, when the free amount for the day is used up, the page simply waits or switches models — it never charges you.

Key is saved in this browser only.

🔒 Your key and your pages never touch our server — there is none. The key is kept only in this browser, and your pages go straight from your computer to Google.

Settings

Google's free limits (as of Nov 3, 2026)

  • Each page = 1 request. Limits count per Google project, per model, per day. The day resets at midnight Pacific time (3 AM New York).
  • Gemini 3.8 Flash (the default, best free model): 20 pages a day. Gemini 3.6 Flash: another 20 a day. The Flash-Lite models allow about 500 a day but are less accurate on hard pages.
  • There is also a per-minute limit; if you hit it, the page waits and continues by itself.
  • Google changes these numbers; your exact current limits are on aistudio.google.com/rate-limit.
  • Free-tier note from Google's pricing page: on the free tier Google may use what you send to improve its products. With billing turned on, it does not. Don't upload private documents on the free tier.
  • For big books: turning on billing in AI Studio costs roughly 1–3 cents per page on Gemini 3.8 Flash (my estimate from Google's paid prices, Nov 2026), and removes the daily cap. That's your choice — this page works fine without it.
