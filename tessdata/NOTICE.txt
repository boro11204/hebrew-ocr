heb_rashi4.traineddata — Tesseract LSTM model for Hebrew Rashi script. Fine-tuned (Nov 2026, hebocr v4) from heb_rashi by Zeev Pekar / pninim.org (https://gitlab.com/pninim.org/tessdata_heb_rashi), Copyright 2021 Zeev Pekar, Apache License 2.0. Modified: further trained on printed lines from public-domain scans (Vilna Shas, Romm 1925, archive.org; Teshuvah MeAhavah, Prague 1815). Training code and numbers: https://github.com/boro11204/hebrew-ocr-offline
heb4.traineddata — Tesseract LSTM model for Hebrew (square script). Fine-tuned (Nov 2026, hebocr v4) from Google's tessdata_best heb (https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best), Apache License 2.0. Modified: further trained on printed Gemara lines from the same public-domain Vilna scans.
words.txt — the 120,000 most frequent words of heb.wordlist from tessdata_heb_rashi (built by its author from Sefaria's open texts), Apache License 2.0; filtered to plain Hebrew-letter words.
